## 💫 About Me

I'm a **Mechatronics Engineer** turned **Backend Python Developer**. I build APIs and complete products with **FastAPI, PostgreSQL and Docker**, and I integrate **AI** into them so they do more than store data: structured outputs, RAG, agents and MCP.

My engineering background is my edge in **Industrial IoT and Industry 4.0**. I've worked on the plant floor as a maintenance technician, so I understand both the machines that generate the data (sensors, PLCs, Modbus, OPC UA) and the systems that process it. I work right where **OT meets IT**.

I'm also an **entrepreneur**: I build software for real businesses with real users, real hardware and real constraints.

- 🛠️ Currently building a **point-of-sale system for butcher shops**: sale by weight, scale and thermal-printer integration, inventory in kg and cash closing (FastAPI · HTMX · SQLAlchemy).
- 🧠 Focused on **applied AI** for small businesses: inventory forecasting, natural-language reports and WhatsApp bots.
- 🌡️ Next: **industrial IoT** with ESP32, MQTT and dashboards for cold-room temperature monitoring.
- ✅ I care about clean architecture, testing, and understanding how things work under the hood — not just making them work.
