# 💫 About Me:

Backend Python Developer with a Mechatronics Engineering background, focused on building robust and scalable APIs with **FastAPI, PostgreSQL and Docker**. My engineering roots give me an edge in **IoT and Industry 4.0**: I understand both the machines that generate the data and the systems that process it. Currently building **Inventario 3D**, a full management platform for my 3D printing business (FastAPI · SQLAlchemy · HTMX · Docker), and working towards the French tech market. I care about clean architecture, testing, and understanding how things work under the hood — not just making them work.
